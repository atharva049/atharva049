## 04 — Citizen science

**Zooniverse · Active Asteroids** — a NASA Partner citizen-science project hosted on Zooniverse.

Volunteers are shown telescope images of known small bodies and asked one careful question: does this object show activity — a tail or a coma? The images come mainly from the Dark Energy Camera on the 4-metre Blanco telescope in Chile, and the project exists because active asteroids are rare and poorly understood, while holding clues about where water in the solar system came from.

What I'm actually doing there:

- Completed the project tutorial and started classifying images
- Practising the discipline of the task: distinguishing real activity from background galaxies, cosmic rays, trailed sources and detector artifacts
- Reading the project's Talk discussions to understand *why* a classification was right or wrong
- Building the habit that matters more than any certificate: **look twice, state what you see, and don't overclaim**

I'm early in this. My contribution right now is careful, honest classification — not discovery claims.
