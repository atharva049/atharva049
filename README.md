## Citizen science

**Zooniverse · Active Asteroids** — a NASA Partner citizen-science project. Volunteers look at telescope images of known small bodies and record whether the object shows activity, such as a tail or coma. The images come mainly from the Dark Energy Camera on the 4-metre Blanco telescope in Chile.

I've completed the tutorial and started classifying. Most of the learning is in telling real activity apart from background galaxies, cosmic rays and image artifacts, and in checking my reasoning against the project's Talk discussions.
